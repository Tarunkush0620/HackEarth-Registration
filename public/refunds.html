Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Notice: The team registration fee (₹400 for standard teams / ₹200 for all-female teams) and optional Stay & Accommodation charges (₹1,500 per person) for WIE HackEarth 2.0 are strictly non-refundable once the transaction is successfully completed.

1. Non-Refundable Registration & Accommodation Fees

Due to the nature of hackathon event planning—where venue capacity, meal arrangements, mentorship resources, event kits, and infrastructural services are booked and committed immediately upon team confirmation—all registration fees are non-refundable and non-transferable once paid.

Accommodation & Shuttle Charges: Similarly, for teams opting for the stay and university shuttle facility (₹1,500 per person), accommodation venues and bus shuttles are reserved and contractually locked with the designated venue well in advance. Therefore, accommodation charges cannot be cancelled, refunded, or prorated once paid.

Please double-check your team's availability and attendance for the offline final on 29–30 Nov 2026 at Galgotias University, Greater Noida before completing the payment.

2. Duplicate Payments & Technical Deduction Errors

In the event that money is deducted from your bank account or UPI application multiple times due to a network glitch or timeout during payment, the following policy applies:

  • Automatic Reversal: Razorpay automatically reconciles duplicate or uncaptured payments. In most cases, banks reverse uncaptured deductions automatically within 5 to 7 business days.
  • Manual Assistance: If an extra deduction is not automatically reversed after 7 business days, please write to us at ieeegusb@galgotiasuniversity.edu.in with:
    • The team name and leader email.
    • The Razorpay Payment ID(s) (format: pay_xxxxxxxxxxxxxx).
    • A screenshot of your bank/UPI transaction debit advice.
    Upon verification with the Razorpay dashboard, the duplicate payment will be refunded directly to the original payment source within 3–5 working days.

3. Disqualification & Fraud

Teams disqualified due to rule violations, code plagiarism, misbehavior, or falsification of details (including claiming the ₹200 All-Women Team discount when one or more members are not female) will not be entitled to any refund.

4. Event Cancellation or Postponement by Organizers

  • In the improbable event that WIE HackEarth 2.0 is officially cancelled in its entirety due to circumstances beyond the organizers' control (force majeure, government restrictions, natural disasters), 100% of the registration fee will be refunded to the original payment source of all confirmed teams.
  • If the event is rescheduled or postponed to new dates, registered teams will have their registrations rolled over to the rescheduled date. Teams unable to attend the new dates will be offered an option to claim a refund upon formal email request within 7 calendar days of the announcement.

5. Contact for Payment Inquiries

For questions or assistance regarding payment status, email us at ieeegusb@galgotiasuniversity.edu.in. We strive to respond within 24 to 48 business hours.
