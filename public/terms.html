Terms & Conditions

Last updated: November 2026

Welcome to WIE HackEarth 2.0 (2026), organized by the IEEE Women in Engineering (WIE) Affinity Group in association with the IEEE Galgotias University Student Branch and IEEE Computational Intelligence Society. By completing the registration and payment process on this website, you and your team agree to abide by these Terms and Conditions.

1. Eligibility & Qualification

  • Registration for the offline final round is exclusively open to teams that qualified through Round 1 on Unstop.
  • Teams must consist of a minimum of 2 and a maximum of 4 members.
  • The team leader must use the exact email address registered during Round 1 on Unstop.
  • All participants must be active students of an accredited educational institution and must present a valid physical college ID card at the event check-in desk.

2. Registration Fee & All-Women Team Discount

  • The standard final-round team registration fee is ₹400 per team.
  • Special WIE All-Women Team Discount: Teams where all participating members (including the team leader) are female qualify for a discounted fee of ₹200 per team (₹200 concession).
  • Strict Anti-Fraud Provision: The ₹200 fee is strictly reserved for genuine all-female teams. Misrepresenting gender details, using false identities, or attempting to exploit this concession constitutes academic fraud. Teams found with inaccurate gender records will be disqualified at the venue desk without a refund.
  • The applicable fee is calculated server-side based on verified details and must be settled in full via Razorpay prior to RSVP pass issuance.

3. Stay, Accommodation & University Shuttle

  • Optional Service: Teams traveling from outside Delhi-NCR may opt for accommodation at a designated venue arranged by Galgotias University.
  • Stay Charges: Accommodation charges are ₹1,500 per person, which covers overnight lodging and arranged university transport.
  • University Shuttle Service: Complimentary shuttle transit is arranged by Galgotias University between the designated accommodation venue and the campus (pick-up before event start and drop back post event), eliminating local commute challenges for outstation teams.
  • Identity & Guest Verification: In accordance with local guest registration laws and university regulations, all participants availing accommodation must present a valid government-issued photo ID (e.g., Aadhaar card, Passport, Voter ID) along with their physical College Student ID upon check-in at the accommodation desk.
  • Conduct & Property Safety: Staying participants must observe all venue rules, designated curfew timings, and campus decorum. Consumption of alcohol, tobacco, or contraband is strictly prohibited. Any damage caused to property or furniture at the stay venue will be recovered in full from the responsible team.

4. Venue Entry & RSVP QR Pass

  • Upon successful payment confirmation, the system generates a cryptographically signed RSVP QR pass bearing the registration code and team name.
  • One RSVP QR pass covers the entire team. The pass must be presented (digitally on a mobile device or printed) at the entrance of Galgotias University during event check-in on 29–30 Nov 2026.
  • Entry will be denied if the QR code signature fails validation or if the registration has not been marked as paid.

5. Code of Conduct & Academic Integrity

  • All participants are expected to maintain professional conduct and respect fellow participants, volunteers, judges, and mentors. Harassment, discrimination, or offensive behavior of any form will result in immediate expulsion from the campus.
  • All code, designs, and prototypes presented during HackEarth 2.0 must be created during the designated hackathon timeline. Pre-built software or plagiarized work will be disqualified.
  • Participants agree to follow all safety and campus rules established by Galgotias University.

  • Neither IEEE, Galgotias University, nor sponsoring organizations claim any equity or ownership over participant projects.

8. Limitation of Liability
