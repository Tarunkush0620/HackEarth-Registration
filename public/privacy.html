Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the organizers of WIE HackEarth 2.0 (2026) (IEEE Women in Engineering Affinity Group and IEEE Galgotias University Student Branch) collect, use, and safeguard personal information submitted by participants during registration, in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) of India.

1. Information We Collect

During team registration, we collect the following personal and team data:

  • Team Details: Team Name, Unstop Team ID, Project/Idea Title, and Selected Track.
  • Leader Contact Information: Full Name, Email Address, 10-digit Mobile Number.
  • Member Information: Full Name, Institutional Email Address, and Gender for each member.
  • Academic & Travel Information: College or University name, City, and Accommodation/Stay requirements (including staying member counts).
  • Payment Transaction Metadata: Razorpay Order ID, Razorpay Payment ID, payment timestamp, and transaction status (we do not store payment card or banking details).

2. Purpose of Data Processing

We process your data strictly for legitimate operational purposes related to WIE HackEarth 2.0:

  • Verifying team eligibility and Round 1 shortlist credentials.
  • Calculating correct team registration fees, including verification of the All-Women Team discount.
  • Generating cryptographic RSVP QR cards for offline check-in and entry desk verification.
  • Organizing university shuttle transit, guest allotments, and check-in rosters for teams opting for stay.
  • Transmitting payment receipts, confirmations, and event schedules via email.
  • Issuing official IEEE participation and winner certificates.

3. Payment Security & Card Data

All financial transactions are processed securely through Razorpay Software Private Limited, an RBI-authorized Payment Aggregator compliant with PCI-DSS (Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard) Level 1. We do not store, process, or have access to your credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, net banking credentials, or UPI PINs. All sensitive financial information is encrypted directly between your browser and Razorpay.

4. Data Sharing & Non-Disclosure

  • We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data to commercial marketers or third-party advertisers.
  • Data is only shared with authorized event infrastructure providers: Supabase (encrypted cloud database hosting), transactional email services strictly for delivering your registration pass and updates, and the designated accommodation venue / transport desk strictly for room allocation and shuttle coordination.

5. Data Retention & Security

We implement security best practices including Row Level Security (RLS) on our database, HTTPS encryption in transit, and restricted administrative key access. Personal records are retained solely for event operations, certificate re-issuance, and compliance, after which they are archived or deleted.

6. Your Rights & Contact

Participants have the right to request review, correction, or deletion of their submitted personal records after the event concludes. For any data inquiries or privacy concerns, please write to us at ieeegusb@galgotiasuniversity.edu.in.
